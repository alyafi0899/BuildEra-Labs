Overview

Not every business fits a standard POS.

Businesses with complex operations juggle flexible product units (pcs, box, meter, kilogram, set, or fully custom — sometimes five different units for one product), deep multi-category inventory, and a mix of retail, wholesale, supplier, and distributor transactions. BuildEra was designed specifically for hardware, building-material, and electronics retailers running out of large shophouses or warehouses, replacing the usual patchwork of POS-for-sales, spreadsheets-for-inventory, and notebooks-for-receivables with one system.

Key Features

Built for how BuildEra is actually used.

01

Flexible Product Units

Every product can carry multiple units of measure — pcs, box, meter, kilogram, set, or a fully custom unit — priced and tracked independently.

02

Real-Time Inventory

Stock movement, restock calculations, and low-stock alerts update automatically as sales and purchases happen.

03

Barcode Generation & Printing

Generate and print custom barcode labels per product, with flexible label and print settings.

04

Receivables & Supplier Management

Track piutang (customer receivables), supplier data, and purchase records in one place instead of a separate notebook.

05

Fast POS Transactions

Barcode scan, cart review, flexible quantity/unit selection, multiple payment methods, and instant invoice print, download, or share.

06

Business Analytics

Turn transaction history into restock guidance and business decisions instead of manual calculation.
