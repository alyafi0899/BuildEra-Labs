# BuildEra Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** October 27, 2026  
**Developer:** Solutivo Labs Indonesia  
**Contact:** solutivolabs@gmail.com

## 1. Introduction

BuildEra is a point-of-sale (POS) and business management application developed by Solutivo Labs Indonesia. It is designed to help businesses manage sales, inventory, purchases, suppliers, receivables, and business reports.

This Privacy Policy explains how BuildEra handles information when you use the application, including information stored locally on your device and information processed through supported cloud services.

By using BuildEra, you acknowledge the practices described in this policy.

## 2. Information We Handle

Depending on the features you use, BuildEra may process the following categories of information:

### A. Business and Transaction Data

Information entered into the application may include:

- Product names, descriptions, prices, stock quantities, and units of measurement.
- Sales transactions, purchase records, and payment information.
- Supplier details and business contact information.
- Customer records and outstanding receivables.
- Business reports and related operational data.

### B. Account Information

If you use account-based features, BuildEra may process information associated with your account, such as your email address and authentication-related identifiers.

Authentication services may process information necessary to create, verify, and maintain your account and support account recovery.

### C. Images and Files

BuildEra may allow you to select images or files for business purposes, including product photos, business logos, and QRIS payment images.

Access to these files is subject to your device's operating system permissions and the file-selection mechanisms used by the application.

### D. Technical and Synchronization Data

When cloud synchronization is enabled, the application may process information required to synchronize business records, maintain synchronization status, and identify pending changes.

Cloud service providers may also process technical information as necessary to provide authentication, storage, synchronization, and related services.

## 3. How Information Is Stored

BuildEra is designed with an offline-first approach.

**Local storage:** Business information may be stored on your device so that supported features remain usable without an internet connection.

**Cloud synchronization:** When cloud features are configured and enabled, supported information may be transmitted to cloud infrastructure to synchronize data between supported application sessions or devices.

The specific information available locally or in the cloud depends on the application version, your configuration, and the features you use.

Local storage does not necessarily mean that all information remains exclusively on your device. Information synchronized to cloud services is subject to the relevant cloud storage and processing arrangements.

## 4. How We Use Information

Information handled by BuildEra may be used to:

- Provide sales, inventory, purchasing, and business management functionality.
- Maintain business records and generate reports.
- Authenticate users and support account-related features.
- Synchronize supported data between local storage and cloud services.
- Maintain application functionality and data consistency.
- Troubleshoot technical problems and improve reliability.
- Protect the application and its data against unauthorized access or misuse.

We do not use business records for purposes unrelated to the application's functionality unless otherwise disclosed or permitted by applicable law.

## 5. Third-Party Services

BuildEra may use third-party infrastructure and services to provide certain features, including authentication, cloud storage, database operations, and synchronization.

Depending on the application version and configuration, these services may include:

- **Supabase:** Authentication, database storage, and related backend functionality.
- **Google Play services:** Application distribution and platform-related functionality, where applicable.

These providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable terms.

Supabase privacy information: https://supabase.com/privacy

Google privacy information: https://policies.google.com/privacy

The third-party services actually used may change as BuildEra evolves.

## 6. Data Sharing

Solutivo Labs Indonesia does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be transmitted to service providers when necessary to operate features that you use, such as account authentication or cloud synchronization.

Information may also be disclosed when required by applicable law, legal process, or a legitimate need to protect the rights, safety, and security of users or the application.

Business information you enter into BuildEra should not be considered publicly available merely because you use the application.

## 7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you delete it, remove the application, or otherwise clear the relevant application data.

Information synchronized to cloud services may remain stored until it is deleted through supported functionality or otherwise removed in accordance with applicable retention procedures and legal requirements.

Deleting local application data does not necessarily delete information already synchronized to cloud services.

If you need assistance with account or cloud data deletion, contact us at solutivolabs@gmail.com and specify your request. Additional information may be required to verify ownership of the relevant account.

Backups, technical logs, or records retained for legitimate operational or legal purposes may not be removed immediately.

## 8. Data Security

We use appropriate technical and organizational measures intended to protect information handled by BuildEra.

However, no method of electronic storage, transmission, or internet-based service can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Users are responsible for protecting their devices, maintaining the confidentiality of their account credentials, and restricting access to sensitive business information.

## 9. Children's Privacy

BuildEra is designed primarily for business and operational use and is not specifically directed at children.

We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe that a child has provided personal information through the application inappropriately, please contact us.

## 10. Your Rights and Choices

Subject to applicable law and the features available in your version of BuildEra, you may be able to:

- Review and manage business information stored in the application.
- Control access to images and files through your device settings.
- Manage your account through supported account features.
- Request assistance with accessing or deleting personal information.

## 12. Subscription Plans and Feature Access

BuildEra offers a Free plan and a paid Pro subscription. Available features are determined by the user's active plan and the features supported by the application version.

### A. Free Plan

The Free plan provides limited access to BuildEra's core point-of-sale functionality, including basic product management, cashier operations, and sales transaction recording.

The Free plan is subject to a limit of three sales transactions. Product management and other available operational features are also restricted compared with the Pro subscription.

The Free plan does not include Excel-based product data import, business analytics, accounts receivable management, or the full purchasing and billing functionality available with Pro.

### B. Pro Subscription

The Pro subscription unlocks the full set of supported BuildEra business management features, including:

- Unlimited product management.
- Full cashier and sales transaction functionality.
- Purchasing and purchase transaction management.
- Billing management.
- Accounts receivable management.
- Business analytics and reporting.
- Excel-based product data import for efficient bulk product registration.
- Other features designated as Pro functionality in the application.

### C. Subscription Pricing

The following standard subscription prices apply, subject to the prices displayed in the purchase interface:

- **Monthly Pro:** IDR 99,000 per month, excluding applicable taxes.
- **Annual Pro:** IDR 999,000 per year, excluding applicable taxes.

The final amount charged may include applicable taxes and is displayed by Google Play before purchase confirmation.

### D. Subscription Management and Access

Paid features are available while the user's Pro subscription is active and recognized by the application.

Subscription purchases, billing, renewals, cancellations, and refund requests are handled according to the applicable Google Play subscription terms.

If a Pro subscription expires or becomes inactive, access to Pro-only features may be restricted and the account may return to Free plan functionality. Existing business data is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy and the application's data retention practices.

Uninstalling BuildEra does not automatically cancel an active subscription.
