# 5. HERO ANIMATION

Design the hero as if it will use high-quality GSAP-style animation.

Initial state:

* white background
* typography slightly blurred
* elements positioned slightly below their final position
* image partially clipped

Entrance animation:

1. Small metadata appears first.
2. Main headline rises upward line-by-line.
3. Blur transitions from approximately 8px to 0px.
4. Image reveals through a mask.
5. Image slowly scales from approximately 1.08 to 1.00.
6. Supporting text fades in.
7. Navigation becomes visible.

The animation must feel slow, controlled and premium.

No bouncing.

No exaggerated easing.
